Cookie policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as the browser's local storage) are small files or pieces of data that a website stores on your device so that it works, or to remember information about your visit.

What Calledoss uses

Calledoss does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It only stores what is strictly necessary for the website to work. This data stays in your browser; it is not sent to Calledoss or anyone else.

NameTypePurposeDuration
calledoss-cookiesNecessary · first-party (local storage)Remembers your cookie choice so we don't ask you again.12 months
calledossKeyNecessary · first-party (session storage)Keeps the private admin panel session open. It only exists if someone logs into that panel.Until the browser is closed

Necessary cookies do not require your consent, under article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE).

Third-party services

Fonts are served from this website itself: there is no connection to Google Fonts. Calendar, results and rankings data is downloaded from our data store on GitHub, which does not set cookies. Links to other websites (RFEA, World Athletics, YouTube broadcasts…) only open if you click them, and from then on those websites' own policies apply.

Analytics and advertising

None are used at the moment. If they are added in the future, they will only be switched on if you accept them in the cookie notice, and this page will be updated to say which services are used, what for and for how long.
